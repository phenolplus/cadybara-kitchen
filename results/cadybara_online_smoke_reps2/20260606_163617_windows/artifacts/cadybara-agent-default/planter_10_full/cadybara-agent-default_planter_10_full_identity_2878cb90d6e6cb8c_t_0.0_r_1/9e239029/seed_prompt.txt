Design a 3D-printable wall-mounted planter with the following specifications.
The body is a hollow cylinder, 80 mm outer diameter, 90 mm tall, with a
uniform 3 mm wall thickness, open at the top and closed at the bottom. The
bottom of the cylinder contains four 3 mm diameter drainage holes arranged
in a square pattern, each centered 15 mm from the cylinder's central axis.
The back is a flat rectangular panel, 100 mm tall x 90 mm wide x 4 mm thick,
tangent to the outside of the cylinder along a vertical line, and extending
5 mm above the cylinder's top edge and 5 mm below its bottom edge. The back
panel contains one keyhole-shaped mounting slot centered horizontally, with
its top edge 15 mm below the top of the back panel: an 8 mm diameter
circular opening with a 4 mm wide x 10 mm tall rectangular slot extending
downward from the circle's center, sized to accept a #8 pan-head screw. All
external edges except the open top rim have a 1 mm fillet. The model is a
single watertight solid suitable for FDM 3D printing without supports when
oriented with the back panel flat on the print bed.
